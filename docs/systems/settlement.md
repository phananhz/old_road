# Settlement

- Version: 0.2
- Status: Draft
- Last updated: 2026-08-14
- Purpose: Define settlement progression direction.

Valen begins as a small medieval village. Player actions can gradually develop it. Future NPC specialists may unlock systems such as carpentry, blacksmithing, tailoring, alchemy, and advanced construction.

The current prototype validates the first cabin construction loop and now includes streamed prototype villages outside the starting map. These villages are deterministic placeholders: they contain harmless NPCs with simple work loops and short interaction lines, but recruitment, trading, housing rules, full dialogue trees, and settlement upgrades remain future scope.
